How to play 🦛
Every day: 7 real films. Guess each one's production budget once, then call how it did worldwide: Flop / Even / Hit / Smash.


🪜 The closer your guess, the higher Pip climbs. Inside the reported budget range (or within 10%) is a bullseye: +11m. Way off and Pip slips.
📣 Right call = +3m. Wrong call = Pip wobbles.


After film 7 Pip dives from the top! Height is your score; more right calls = a prettier dive (swan 🦢 · cannonball 💣 · belly flop 💦).
