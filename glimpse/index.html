How to play
  1. Three worlds a day. Each one: a busy picture appears for 10 seconds (world 3 is a touch quicker: 8).
  2. Remember. It hides. You get 60 seconds to type everything you saw, one at a time. "Cup and plate" counts for both.
  3. Chips tell you instantly: ✓ found ✗ not there too vague
  4. Score: every find scores points. Harder things score more: ⚪ Common 50 · 🔷 Uncommon 80 · ✨ Rare 120 · 💎 Epic 180. Your three worlds add up to a daily total. Same worlds for everyone, so you can compare with friends. Finished? Try the optional ⭐ Bonus world.
Aussie, UK and US names all work: esky, cooler, chilly bin. Typos are fine. 🎵 music and 🔔 sound effects can be muted up top.
