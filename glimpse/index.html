How to play
  1. Look at the scene for 10 seconds. Try to remember all the objects.
  2. The scene hides. You have 60 seconds to type everything you saw. Type one object, then press Enter.
  3. There are 3 scenes each day. Scene 3 is a bit harder: you only get 8 seconds to look.
  4. Small or hidden objects score more points: ⚪ 50 · 🔷 80 · ✨ 120 · 💎 180. These are our best guess at how hard each object is.
  5. Your 3 scenes add up to your score for the day. Everyone gets the same scenes, so you can compare with friends.
  6. Want more? Play the ⭐ bonus scene. You get 7 seconds to look and 45 seconds to type. It doesn't change your daily score.
Typos are fine. Aussie, UK and US words all work, like esky, cooler and chilly bin.
The chips tell you how you went: ✓ found ✗ not in the scene be more specific
Privacy: when you finish a scene, the game sends your score and which objects you found, with a random ID made on this device. No name, email or location. We use it to work out real rarity and how you compare with other players. Rarity says "(est.)" until 30 people have played a scene that day.
