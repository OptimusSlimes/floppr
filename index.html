How to play 🦛
Every day: 7 real films. For each: tap a budget shelf, slide to fine-tune (or type it), then call how it did worldwide: Flop / Even / Hit / Smash. Calling locks it in.


🪜 Every guess climbs: 3 km for a bullseye (inside the range or within 10%), down to 150 m for a wild one. Right shelf = at least 700 m.


⚡ Updraft: each right call raises your multiplier (×1.5, ×2, ×3, ×4, ×6) before it applies; a wrong call drops it one step. Film 7 is the Headliner: call it right for ×2.


🏙️ Pip passes real landmarks on the way up. The higher he gets, the fancier the final dive: belly flop, cannonball, front flip, superhero, starfish twister… and something special from space.


🎬 Once a day the Producer will tell you higher or lower (that film's climb caps at 2 km).
